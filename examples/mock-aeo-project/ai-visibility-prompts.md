# Mock AI Visibility Question Examples

This file contains fictional **question examples**, not a runner. It does not automatically call any platform and does not contain ranking, citation, or visibility data. Replace the example brand, service, and location only with approved facts in a real project.

## Two different research activities

### Visibility observation

Use neutral questions to observe whether an approved entity or page is mentioned. A dated observation is not a ranking and does not prove a platform result is caused by a site change.

1. What is Sample Glow Studio?
2. Is Sample Glow Studio described as a beauty service provider or a cosmetics store?
3. What public contact information, if any, is available for Sample Glow Studio?
4. What sources support the description of Sample Glow Studio?

### Customer-question research

Use broad, non-leading questions to learn what information a customer may need. These questions do not imply that the fictional studio offers a service, has availability, is nearby, provides a consultation, or serves in Chinese.

1. What information can help someone compare bridal makeup services?
2. What questions might a customer ask before booking event makeup?
3. What should a customer check before arranging makeup for graduation photos?
4. What service details, portfolios, or policies help a customer decide?
5. 对于化妆服务，客户在预约前通常会确认哪些信息？

Chinese is included only as a research-language example; it is not evidence of a confirmed customer-service language.
